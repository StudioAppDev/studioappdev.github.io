UAC & AdMob Manager
campaigns and ROAS, in one place.

A desktop app for our own studio to launch Google Ads App campaigns across many countries, manage ad videos, and see return on ad spend by country using AdMob revenue — with every change previewed before it is sent.

What it does Privacy Policy

What it does

Built for app marketers who run App campaigns in many countries.

Multi-country campaigns

Plan App campaigns for 20+ countries in a spreadsheet-style grid, validate with Google first, then create them — always paused, never duplicated.

Ad video library

Organize ad videos by country and aspect ratio, upload them through the Google Ads API, and attach them to campaigns.

ROAS by country

Combine Google Ads spend with AdMob ad revenue per country, with alerts for low ROAS, high CPI and unusual traffic.

Your Google data stays on your computer

The app runs on the user's own computer and has no server. It connects directly to Google's APIs with the user's permission, stores reports locally, and encrypts sign-in tokens with the operating system's keychain.

Data is never sold or shared, and is used only to provide the features above. Read the full Privacy Policy.
